Before you begin: think back to who you were up to the age of 21.
Answer the way you naturally thought, spoke and behaved then, not how you behave today. As we grow, most of us consciously work on our weaker sides and become more balanced: the quiet person learns to speak up, the impulsive one learns to plan. That growth is valuable, but it hides our natural preference. Your true type shows most clearly in your thinking and actions before age 21.
There are no right or wrong answers. Pick the option that fits you most of the time, even if both feel partly true.
